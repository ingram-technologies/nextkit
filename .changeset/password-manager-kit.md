Pieces that make sign-in work with password managers: `usePasskeyAutofill` and `PASSKEY_AUTOFILL_EMAIL` for passkeys in the autofill dropdown, `totpEnrollment` (QR path, `otpauth://` link, grouped key) for 2FA setup, and `passkeyEndpoints` / `changePasswordRedirect` route handlers at `./well-known`.
